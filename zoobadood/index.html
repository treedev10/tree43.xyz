What is Zoobadood?

Zoobadood is a real game people play with a stick. You stand in a circle, throw it across, and whoever it comes to has to catch it by the right end. This is that game in VR, with a sword.

It's a party game for 2 to 10 people. Rounds are quick, and the last one with an arm left wins.

How it plays

1 · Catch the handle

Grab the yellow part

When the sword comes your way, grip it by the yellow handle and you're fine.

2 · Miss and lose an arm

Two arms, two mistakes

Grab the blade, or let a throw that comes at you get past, and that arm is gone. Lose both and you sit out the rest of the round.

3 · Throw it fair

Or throw it again

Too high, too far past the circle, or nowhere near anyone, and you have to throw again. Throw as hard as you want, but throw it at someone.

What's in it

Online, 2 to 10 players

Press Play and you're put in a public game with other people.

Private rooms

Make a room and give your friends the 6-character code. Nobody else can get in.

Practice mode

The sword gets thrown at you over and over until catching it feels normal.

Real throwing

Swing your arm and let go. The harder you swing, the faster it goes.

Text chat

Send a quick phrase in one tap, or type on the in-game keyboard. Messages pop up over your head.

Your Meta name

Everyone sees your Meta display name above your head and in the player list.

FAQ

What do I need to play?

A Meta Quest headset with its Touch controllers. Hand tracking isn't supported, because catching uses the grip button.

When is it coming out?

It's coming to the Meta Horizon Store. There's no release date yet. This page will be updated when there is.

Do I need the internet?

Only for online games. Practice works offline.

How much room do I need?

You don't walk around, so standing in one spot is enough. Just make sure you can swing your arm without hitting anything.

Is there chat?

Yes, text chat: quick phrases or the in-game keyboard, in online games. Messages go only to the players in your game and aren't stored, and you can mute or report anyone from the player list. Voice chat is planned for a later update. The privacy policy explains how both work.

What name do other players see?

Your Meta display name (or your Meta username if you haven't set one). The game doesn't use anything else from your Meta account.

Is it violent?

Not really. When you lose an arm, your hand fades out. There's no blood or injury shown.

How do I report someone?

Open the player list (PLAYERS on your panel, or B on your controller), pick the player and choose REPORT. You can also use the report option in your Quest's menu. More detail is in the privacy policy.

What if someone is ruining the game?

Mute them from the player list to hide their chat, or start a vote kick from the same list. If most of the other players agree, they're removed from the room and can't come back to it. You can also report them, or play in a private room with people you know.
